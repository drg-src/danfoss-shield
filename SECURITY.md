# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability in this tool

If you find a security issue in danfoss-shield itself, please report it privately rather than
opening a public issue. Please include what you did, what you expected, and what happened. We aim to acknowledge within a
reasonable time and to credit reporters who want it.

## Reporting an exposed controller you found (not a bug in this tool)

This tool may help you notice that a refrigeration controller is exposed on the internet. **If it is
not yours, do not probe, access, or modify it.**

1. Try to reach the owner/operator of the system directly.
2. If you can't, report it through a responsible-disclosure channel - the device vendor's security
   contact, or your national CERT/CSIRT.
3. Give the owner/vendor reasonable time to act before any public discussion, and never publish a
   working path that only enables harm.

## Scope and intent

danfoss-shield is a defensive stopgap, intended **only** for systems you own or are explicitly
authorized to test. Unauthorized scanning or access may be illegal. See the "Legal & intended use"
section of the README.
